# Mini Style Lab design direction

## Shared brief

Show that a style is a material vocabulary serving a content-specific spatial idea. Every micro-site must remain recognizable with effects disabled, work at desktop/tablet/mobile widths, and include one accessible stateful interaction.

## Directions

### 01 — Morrow Editions

- Brand truth: opening a new book changes how the reader enters a world.
- Thesis: the book physically opens the page; its spine is the alignment hinge.
- Object role: anchor and reveal.
- Material budget: paper, ink, one vermilion signal; no decorative depth.

### 02 — Rivet FM

- Brand truth: live radio is one continuous public frequency.
- Thesis: the frequency line is the page axis and the active broadcast state.
- Object role: utility and bridge.
- Material budget: black, off-white, safety yellow; motion only for live state.

### 03 — Luma Loop

- Brand truth: a roller-rink night is a shared loop through time.
- Thesis: the skate route is also the session schedule.
- Object role: anchor, utility, and reveal.
- Material budget: one chrome loop, coral/cyan state signals, glow limited to the route.

### 04 — Kin Ferment

- Brand truth: fermentation is patient, visible biological time.
- Thesis: time rises through the page like bubbles in a jar.
- Object role: anchor and utility.
- Material budget: paper, forest ink, mustard and tomato; depth only on the jar.

## Shared anti-patterns

- No generic split hero.
- No repeated card or bento grid.
- No surface-effect substitution for composition.
- No objects that can be removed without changing alignment, narrative, or action.
- Mobile must recompose the signature relationship rather than simply stack columns.
