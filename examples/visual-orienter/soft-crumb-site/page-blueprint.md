# Page blueprint

1. **Arrival — promise.** Large title begins the reading axis. The croissant crosses that axis and turns toward the paper fold.
2. **Constraint — pickup.** The fold reveals Saturday/Sunday hours and Friday cutoff before the visitor reaches products.
3. **Choice — today.** Three pastries appear as one editorial lineup with real quantity controls, not product cards.
4. **Proof — process.** One rust field changes density and explains why the product takes time.
5. **Closure — reservation.** Operational details and a real bag preview resolve the paper motif into an order slip.

Effect-off proof: title scale, overlap, fold silhouette, editorial rules, rust interruption, and receipt geometry establish the hierarchy without glow, glass, gradients, or animation.

Primary responsive breakpoint behavior:

- Above 1050px: full asymmetric composition and navigation.
- 761–1050px: simplified header and enlarged product-to-title overlap.
- 760px and below: cropped upper-right hero object, two-column product flow, stepped process rows, bottom bag utility.
