# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Design-conscious neighborhood customers choosing a small weekend treat, a breakfast box, or a gift for someone nearby.

## Product Purpose

Introduce Soft Crumb as a small-batch bakery with a playful material identity, make today’s selection easy to understand, and move visitors toward reserving a pickup box.

## Positioning

Croissants with soft edges and a sharp point of view.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: reserve a pickup box.
- Secondary CTA: browse today’s bake.
- Memory line: “Tomorrow’s softest hour, baked today.”
- Belief ladder: the work is crafted by hand; flavors are distinctive but approachable; pickup is simple.
- Proof on hand: daily limited counts, 36-hour dough process, pickup hours and address.

## Brand Personality

Tactile, bright, and assured. Warmth comes through handmade texture and language, while blueberry color and gallery-like composition prevent familiar beige-bakery styling.

## Anti-references

Beige farmhouse cafés, chalkboard nostalgia, pink cupcake shops, identical rounded cards, fake testimonials, generic luxury serif branding, and literal clay blobs applied to every surface.

## Design Principles

- Make the pastry the sculpture.
- Keep ordering language simple and specific.
- Let saturated color carry the brand.
- Use clay depth selectively, not as a skin on every component.
- Recompose the visual story for mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard-complete ordering controls, visible focus, reduced-motion support, descriptive image text, and touch targets of at least 44px.
