# Quality and proof

Acceptance requires:

- primary tasks work;
- the requested operation and scope are respected;
- approved sections and unrelated files remain intact;
- the page is coherent at desktop, tablet when required, and mobile;
- keyboard, focus, touch, contrast, zoom, long content, loading, error, empty, and reduced-motion behavior are verified where applicable;
- assets have intentional roles and truthful provenance;
- automatic decorative assets are absent;
- visual hierarchy survives with effects removed;
- source changes produce a meaningful rendered change;
- unresolved proof is reported as unverified, not hidden.

Use screenshots and interaction evidence to judge the rendered product. DOM geometry and source scans support visual judgment but do not replace it.

Preserve references by relationships: focal position, headline measure, scale contrast, whitespace, crop, alignment, rhythm, and responsive transformation. Correct inaccessible or impossible reference details instead of copying them blindly.
