# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-19)

First release of Lagann as an Agent Skill (`skills/lagann/`).

- Mode router: Create, Modify, Redesign, Audit, Continuous QA, with effort levels (Fast, Balanced, Deep).
- Four stage modules: Discover, Imagine, Construct, Verify.
- Anti-slop module: three orders of slop, five questions, evidence tests, symptom catalogue with the
  conditions under which each pattern is legitimate, verdicts, a preserve-first rule.
- Specialist registry and conflict rulings for impeccable, taste-skill, frontend-design, ui-ux-pro-max,
  emil-design-eng, animate, apple-design, designing-user-experiences, web-design-guidelines,
  redesign-skill, soft-skill, minimalist-skill and the motion-review skills.
- Per-project `.design-lagann/context.md` and a personal `~/.design-lagann/profile.md`.
- Craft core (`references/craft.md`): mechanics defaults with reasons, mode-aware, so Lagann works with
  no specialist installed. A direction method of its own (rut first, three candidates from different
  subject facts, differing on at least three axes) and a fallback table for every specialist.
- Worked examples (`references/examples.md`).
- Plan-before-build in proportion to scope, reference acquisition with one role per source, complete
  product states, bounded repair passes that stop when nothing changes.
- Scripts: `context-scan.mjs`, `inspect.mjs` (tiered wrapper around taste-skill's inspector),
  `browser-checks.js`.
- Evals: 9 behaviour evals, 20 trigger queries and fixtures in `evals/`; `scripts/validate.mjs` and CI
  check the skill and the evals.
- `docs/comparison.md`: rubric comparison with the design skills Lagann orchestrates.
